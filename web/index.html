Record, split and train your voice.

Record as long as you like, trim it, even out the volume and save MP3s split into 5, 10 or 25 MB parts that fit any upload limit. The Voice tab is a full voice-training coach for feminine or masculine voice goals: live pitch in Hz, resonance, vocal weight, 25 guided exercises for each goal and progress over time.

What it does

Record

No time limit

Record from the microphone with a brass loudness dial. Decide afterwards how to cut and split it.

Split

MP3 parts that fit

Pick 5, 10, 25 MB or your own size. Every part stays under the limit, ready for mail and chat apps.

Clean up

Trim and even out

Drag the handles to trim. "Even out volume" lifts quiet speech and holds loud peaks down.

Voice

Live pitch in Hz

A 10 Hz scale with male and female ranges and your own target line, plus resonance, weight, volume and breathiness meters.

Train

Feminine or masculine goal

Switch between voice feminization (MtF) and masculinization (FtM). Each has 25 guided exercises — glides, resonance and weight drills, melody, reading and mimicry — and its own routine.

Track

Reports and progress

Session reports with note names, notes, playback and PDF export. Daily score, streaks, steps and achievements.

Install

Android (Samsung and others)

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open Recorder.apk from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then tap Install.

Files are saved in Music/Recorder, PDF reports in Download/Recorder.

iPhone

  1. Open the web app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Recorder from the Home Screen and allow the microphone when asked.

Works offline after the first visit. Save MP3s and PDFs with the share button → Save to Files.

Privacy

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no server and no tracking. Recordings, voice sessions and notes are stored only on your device, and nothing is uploaded unless you share a file yourself. Voice measurements are estimates from the phone microphone, not medical advice.
